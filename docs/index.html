Method

Method notes

  • No API keys. EDGAR requires only a User-Agent header; FRED is read through the public fredgraph.csv endpoint; the vast.ai marketplace is public.
  • Cash-flow XBRL values are year-to-date; quarterly figures are derived by differencing within the fiscal year.
  • Companies differ in tagging (Amazon books capex under PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets); tags are tried in fallback order.
  • Extraction keeps its evidence: the depreciation table stores the verbatim sentence and filing URL for every value, so nothing rests on the parser alone.
  • Curated inputs are few and cited. AI revenue has no single official series, and private credit deals are not all in public filings; each curated row carries its source.

Sources: SEC EDGAR (XBRL company facts; 10-K text), FRED (fredgraph.csv), vast.ai public marketplace. Curated inputs carry citations in data/curated. Typeset in the design of The Price of Thinking. © Allan Pedersen · MIT licence.
